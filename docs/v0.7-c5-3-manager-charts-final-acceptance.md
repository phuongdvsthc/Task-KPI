# v0.7-C5.3 – Manager Dashboard Charts Final Acceptance

## Overview
v0.7-C5.3 establishes final acceptance verification for all Manager Dashboard charts implemented in v0.7-C5.1 (Operational Charts) and v0.7-C5.2 (Metric & KPI Charts):
1. **Task-Status Comparison (Bar Chart)**: Mutually exclusive task statuses (`Đã hoàn thành`, `Đang thực hiện`, `Chưa bắt đầu`) with auxiliary overdue and without-due-date badges.
2. **Daily-Report Trend (Line Chart)**: Chronological reporting progression in employee-days (`Lượt ngày đã báo cáo`).
3. **Metric Trend (Line Chart & Selector)**: Time-series values for authorized metrics with unit and aggregation preservation.
4. **KPI Period by Period (Bar Chart)**: Comparison of official (`Chính thức`) or provisional (`Tạm tính`) KPI scores across discrete evaluation periods.

## Acceptance Audit Results
- **Aggregate Sources**: 100% powered by backend-aggregated responses (`UnifiedDashboardResponse`), with zero paginated C4 table rows or frontend scoring calculations.
- **Scope & Filters**: Fully synchronized with date range, unit selection, and employee filter. Cleared filters return to permitted manager scope without system-wide retrieval.
- **No Ranking**: Strictly free of employee ranking, top/bottom lists, unit comparisons, and grade scoring.
- **UI States**: Independent empty, partial, and error states across all four chart domains.
- **Responsive & Accessibility**: Responsive Recharts containers, semantic headings, descriptive labels, and noninteractive visual elements.
- **Read-Only**: Zero business data writes, task modifications, report submissions, or KPI updates caused by chart rendering or interaction.
- **Regression & Build**: All v0.7-A, Staff dashboard, Team monitoring, and C5 self-tests pass successfully. Production build passes cleanly.
