# v0.7-B5 – Staff Dashboard Final Acceptance Report

## Overview
v0.7-B5 provides a comprehensive automated final acceptance check of the complete Staff Dashboard (v0.7-B1 through v0.7-B4) backed by v0.7-A1 through v0.7-A5 reporting services.

## Acceptance Audit Results
1. **Staff Authorization**: Active Staff are correctly scoped to personal data. Unauthorized users, inactive users, and cross-employee requests are securely rejected (401/403).
2. **Filter Behavior**: Valid date ranges and reporting periods are accepted and passed correctly. Invalid ranges are rejected with 400. Stale filter protection is enforced.
3. **Summary Cards**: Task, Daily Report, KPI, and Attention cards render accurate counts and states without arbitrary thresholds or frontend recalculations.
4. **Operational & Performance Charts**: Recharts BarChart and LineChart render valid task distributions and daily-report trends, along with accessible metric trends and KPI period results.
5. **Attention & Navigation**: Factual attention items map cleanly to Vietnamese labels and safe existing routes (`#/tasks`, `#/daily-reports`, `#/kpis`, `#/metrics`) without exposing sensitive tokens or internal codes.
6. **Read-Only Guarantees**: Dashboard rendering, refreshing, and navigation trigger zero write operations or side effects.
7. **UI States & Responsiveness**: Loading, empty, partial, and error states are handled gracefully across all viewports (mobile, tablet, desktop) with no horizontal overflow.
8. **Accessibility**: Semantic headings, ARIA attributes, keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, and textual status indicators are fully validated.
