## Security Guarantees
1. **No System Role Reliance**: B1 capability checks and data scope resolution supersede all legacy `profiles.system_role` and `authenticateAdmin` checks.
2. **Executive Restriction**: Executives possess `all` view scope but lack mutation capabilities (`admissions.finalize`, `admissions.reopen`, `admissions.manage_sheets`, `admissions.sync_sheets`), resulting in immediate 403 on all mutation routes.
3. **Secret Masking & Protection**: Private keys and service account secrets are never exposed in responses, logged, or recorded in audit history.
4. **Explicit Sync Safeguards**: Đồng bộ chỉ chạy khi người dùng xác nhận rõ (no automatic background full-file sync).
